About

Yay Chann is a policy researcher and devoted policy advocate, who worked as a senior policy analyst at Another Development (AD), a local policy think tank, and also worked as a research assistant at Chiang Mai University School of Public Policy, Thailand and Forest Research Institute, Nay Pyi Taw, Myanmar.

He has experience in policy analysis, urban, and local governance research and advocacy. As a policy researcher, he contributed three policy papers for his organisation — AD, one working paper for East Asia Institute in Seoul, Korea, and three policy briefs for Chiang Mai University School of Public Policy, Thailand.

His opinion pieces can also be found at Tea Circle Oxford and The Myanmar Times. More than 15 media outlets including the World Economic Forum covered his research findings on green spaces in Yangon; his work was also discussed at a public debate hosted by the Institute for Strategy and Policy, Myanmar.

He studied Research Studies at Yangon University of Economics, and holds an M.A. in Public Policy at Chiang Mai University School of Public Policy. He is currently completing his PhD at the school.
